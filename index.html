User Manual

The Problem Solving Workbook — Companion App

Everything you need to work through all 61 pages, use the calculators, build a PDF report, and keep your work safe.

Creed to Commerce™ · The Balanced Middle Path Academy, LLC

1Welcome

The Problem Solving Workbook App is the interactive companion to the 61-page PDF workbook. It keeps the same pages, the same numbering and the same worksheets, and adds the things a PDF cannot do: your answers save automatically, scores and money figures calculate for you, progress is tracked, and a professional PDF report can be built from what you have written.

What stays the same

Page order and numbers (1–61), eight sections, 18 core worksheets, 14 reflection prompts, a 30-day action plan and six frameworks.

What the app adds

Autosave, quiz scoring, five live calculators, a progress bar, sample data, undo, backup/restore and PDF reports.

What you need

Any modern browser on a phone, tablet or computer. No account, no download and no sign-up.

Your privacy in one lineEverything you type stays in this browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded to any server. See Your data & privacy.

2Quick start (5 minutes)

  1. Open the app and tap TAP TO BEGIN → on the cover. On a return visit you will also see Continue where I left off.
  2. Read pages 4–5 (Welcome and How To Use This Workbook), then go to the Quiz tab.
  3. Answer the ten statements on pages 7–8. Be honest about your average behaviour, not your best week.
  4. Read your score on page 9. The app shows your profile and links straight to the worksheets it recommends.
  5. Work the recommended worksheets in the Worksheets tab. Type in any box; it saves by itself.
  6. Tick your first moves in the Action tab (pages 47–49) and set your Day 1 and Day 30 dates on page 46.
  7. Build a report any time: ☰ Tools → Build PDF report.
Want to see it working first?Open ☰ Tools → Load sample data. A filled-in example appears so you can explore the scores, calculators and report. When you are done, choose Clear sample data — it removes only what the sample added and leaves your own entries untouched.

4Moving around

Four ways to get to a page

  • Section pills — Welcome, Quiz, Goals, Worksheets, Reflect, Action, Frameworks, Bonus.
  • Prev / Next — follows the printed workbook order.
  • Page menu — pick any of the 61 pages from the list.
  • Table of Contents (page 3) — every card is tappable and shows a small completion bar for its section.

Result cards on the score page and worksheet cross-references (for example “Constraint Finder → p.26”) are also links.

Bookmarkable page links

Each page has its own address ending in #p= and the page number, for example …/#p=17 opens Worksheet 04, the Cash Flow Reality Check. Add it to your browser bookmarks, or share it with a client who already has the app.

Coming back laterThe app reopens on the cover. Tap Continue where I left off to return to the page you were on.

Autosave

The app saves a moment after you stop typing, and again when you leave the page. There is no Save button to press. If you close the browser mid-sentence, reopen the app and your text will be there.

ImportantSaves live in this browser on this device. Clearing the browser's site data, using a private/incognito window, or switching to a different browser or device will show an empty workbook. Use Export backup regularly.

Anything can be changed

Every line, box, checkbox and score can be edited at any time. Typed over a calculated number? Clear the box and the app goes back to calculating it for you.

7The live calculators

Calculators appear inside the worksheet where the PDF had a blank grid. Type your numbers in the boxes above each result panel; the panel updates immediately. Results use the currency set under Settings.

Cash Flow Reality Check — Worksheet 04 (page 17)

Enter average monthly revenue, total fixed costs, variable costs as a percentage of revenue, and what you actually paid yourself. The panel shows your break-even point per month and your cushion (revenue above break-even; a negative number means a shortfall). Then write what you assumed versus what the numbers say.

Reading the resultBreak-even = (fixed costs + what you pay yourself) ÷ (1 − variable-cost %). Owner's pay is counted as a cost, so break-even shows the revenue needed to cover the business and you. If revenue sits close to it, a single slow month can put you under water.

Profit-First Split — Worksheet 08 (page 21)

Enter percentages for Profit, Owner's Pay, Tax and Operating Expenses. A meter shows the total allocated; aim for exactly 100%. The panel tells you if you are over or how much is unallocated. Under “Try it on a deposit of”, enter any deposit amount to see how much goes to each slice in your chosen currency.

Emergency Buffer Builder — Worksheet 09 (page 22)

Enter your bare-bones monthly survival number, how many months of buffer you want, and what you hold today. The panel shows the buffer target (survival number × months), still to build and % funded with a progress meter.

Debt Detangler — Worksheet 10 (page 23)

List up to three debts, each with lender, balance, interest rate and minimum payment. The panel totals your balance, monthly minimums and average rate, then suggests two orders:

  • Avalanche — highest interest rate first (cheapest overall).
  • Snowball — smallest balance first (quickest early wins).

Decision Matrix — Framework 03 (page 52)

Name up to three options and score each from 1 to 5 on Cost, Profit impact, Speed and Fits my values. The totals table ranks the options. Treat the highest total as a conversation starter, then adjust if your gut disagrees and write why.

Other live helpersThe Twenty-Solutions Sprint (page 28) counts your ideas and starred favourites. The quiz page tallies as you answer. The Action Plan pages show moves completed.

8The 30-Day Action Plan (pages 46–49)

Eighteen moves in three phases of ten days: Stabilise (days 1–10), Systemise (days 11–20) and Strengthen (days 21–30).

  1. On page 46, set My Day 1 and My Day 30 and write your accountability move (who you will tell).
  2. On each phase page, tick a move when it is done.
  3. Add a By when? date and a First tiny step for each move. Tiny means doable in five minutes.

Each page shows moves completed so far (for example “2 of 18”), and the same count appears in the report summary.

9Weekly CFO Hour and your log (pages 31 and 55)

Page 31 (Worksheet 18) sets up your weekly hour. Page 55 (Framework 06) is the checklist you repeat every week.

  1. Enter Week of and your Cash position today.
  2. Tick through the eight checklist items and write one honest paragraph on what you noticed.
  3. Tap Save this week to my log & start a fresh checklist. The week is stored with its date, items ticked, cash figure and note, and the checklist resets for next week.

Your saved weeks are listed under the checklist and appear as a Weekly CFO log table in the PDF report.

11PDF reporting

The report turns your answers into a clean, branded document you can keep, share with an accountant or coach, or bring to a review. It reads live from your workbook, so rebuild it whenever your answers change.

Build a report

  1. Tap PDF Report in the top bar, or ☰ Tools → Build PDF report. (The PDF report button at the foot of each page does the same.)
  2. Use the checkboxes to choose what to include:
    • Executive summary
    • Self-assessment
    • Goals
    • Core worksheets
    • Reflections
    • 30-Day action plan
    • Frameworks
    • Final review & next steps
    • Notes
    • Answered fields only — leaves out blank prompts so the report stays short.
  3. Choose how to save it (below).
  4. Tap ← Back to workbook when finished.

What the report contains

  • Cover block with the Basmala, title, prepared-for line (your business name from Settings) and report date.
  • Executive summary tiles: workbook completion, self-assessment score, profile, break-even per month, Profit-First split, buffer funded, total debt and 30-day moves done, plus a completion-by-section table and “priorities at a glance”.
  • Self-assessment table of all ten statements, your answers and points, with your total and profile (and Day-1 baseline if locked).
  • Worksheet answers as question-and-answer pairs, with calculator results shown as result tiles and tables.
  • Action plan tables by phase, the Decision Matrix table, and the Weekly CFO log.
  • A Creed to Commerce™ footer with the credit line and an educational-use disclaimer.

Two ways to save

Print / Save as PDF

Opens your browser's print dialog. Choose Save as PDF (or your printer) as the destination. This works offline and gives the best text quality, including the Arabic Basmala.

Download PDF file

Creates a PDF file and downloads it directly. It fetches a small helper library the first time, so you need an internet connection. If it cannot load, the app falls back to the print dialog.

Print dialog tipsSet paper to A4 or Letter, margins to Default, and switch Background graphics on so the brown section bars and gold rules appear. On phones, choose “Save as PDF” in the share/print sheet.

13Your data & privacy

Where your answers live

The app has no server and no account. Your work is stored in your browser's local storage on the device you are using, under a single entry named bmpa_problem_solving_workbook_v1. Nothing is sent to the Balanced Middle Path Academy or anyone else.

GoodPrivate by design, works offline, no log-in to forget.
Trade-offYour data does not follow you between devices or browsers, and can be lost if site data is cleared. Back up.

Back up and restore

  1. Export: ☰ Tools → Export backup (.json). A file is saved to your downloads. Keep it somewhere safe (cloud drive, email to yourself).
  2. Import: on the new device, open the app, then Import backup (.json) and choose the file. The app asks before replacing what is currently there.
When to exportAfter each work session, before clearing browser data, before changing phones or computers, and before using Erase everything I typed.

Shared computers

If more than one person uses the same browser profile they will see the same workbook. Use separate browser profiles, or export and then erase when you finish.

15Install & offline use

The app is a Progressive Web App. After the first visit it caches itself, so it keeps working without internet (the PDF download button and web fonts are the only parts that need a connection).

DeviceHow to add it to your home screen / apps
iPhone / iPad (Safari)Tap Share → Add to Home Screen.
Android (Chrome)Tap the ⋮ menu → Install app or Add to Home screen.
Windows / Mac / Chromebook (Chrome, Edge)Click the install icon at the right of the address bar, or the browser menu → Install.
UpdatesWhen you publish a new version, the app checks the network first, so users receive changes the next time they open it online. If an old version seems stuck, refresh the page once or twice.

17Deploy: GitHub → Vercel

For the owner or administrator publishing the app. No build tools are needed — the app is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

Files in the project

FilePurpose
index.htmlThe complete app (all pages, calculators, reports).
manual.htmlThis user manual (link it from your sales page or onboarding email).
manifest.webmanifest, favicon.svgInstall-to-home-screen details and icon.
sw.jsOffline support (service worker; network-first so updates arrive quickly).
vercel.jsonClean URLs and security headers for Vercel.
README.md, .gitignoreRepo housekeeping.

Steps

  1. Create a new GitHub repository (for example problem-solving-workbook-app).
  2. From the unzipped project folder, run: 
    git init
git add .
git commit -m "Initial release"
git branch -M main
git remote add origin https://github.com/YOUR-ACCOUNT/problem-solving-workbook-app.git
git push -u origin main
  3. In Vercel choose Add New → Project and import the repository.
  4. Set Framework Preset to Other. Leave Build Command, Output Directory and Install Command empty. Click Deploy.
  5. Open the Vercel address. The app is at / and the manual at /manual.
  6. Optional: add your own domain under Settings → Domains.

Updating later

Edit the files, git commit and git push. Vercel redeploys automatically. Users' saved answers are not affected by an update.

Before you publishReplace the placeholder links on the cover and footer with your own (Payhip, website, Kit sign-up), and check that the title page text and credit line read the way you want.

18Troubleshooting & FAQ

My answers disappeared.

They are stored in the browser you were using. Check you are on the same browser, the same device, the same web address and not in a private window. If you cleared site data, restore from an exported backup (Your data).

Can I use the app on my phone and my laptop?

Yes, but each device has its own copy. Export on one and Import on the other to carry your work across. The last import replaces the data on that device.

The Download PDF button didn't work.

That button needs internet the first time to fetch its helper library. Use Print / Save as PDF instead, which works offline. Also check that pop-up or download blocking isn't enabled for the site.

The printed report has no colours.

In the print dialog, turn on Background graphics (Chrome/Edge) or Print backgrounds (Firefox/Safari).

The Arabic Basmala looks different or missing.

It uses the Amiri web font, which loads online. Offline, your device's default Arabic font is used — still correct, just styled differently.

A calculator shows a dash instead of a number.

It needs all of its inputs. Fill every box above the result panel (for example, revenue, fixed costs and variable-cost % for break-even). Variable costs must be below 100%, or there is no room left to cover fixed costs.

The Profit-First total isn't 100%.

Adjust the four percentages until they add up. The total panel shows how far off you are.

Sample data got mixed with my own answers.

Choose Clear sample data. It removes only the fields the sample filled. Anything you typed afterwards, even over a sample value, stays.

The app shows an old version after an update.

Refresh once or twice with a connection. If it persists, clear this site's cached files in the browser (not site data, or you will lose your answers — export first to be safe).

Does the app share my financial figures?

No. Nothing leaves your device unless you export or print it yourself.

Can my clients use this with me in a session?

Yes. Have them open the app on their own device, or use Print a blank workbook for paper sessions. Ask them to export a backup at the end so they keep their work.
